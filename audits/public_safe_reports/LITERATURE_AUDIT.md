## Source and exact scope

- Alexis de Colnet's Artois University thesis, *Hard Functions in Knowledge Compilation: from Lower Bounds to Applications* (2022), Definition 34, printed p. 53 (PDF p. 65), defines strong error relative to the true model mass. Section 2.5, Open Question 1, printed p. 62 (PDF p. 74), asks for exponential size of all strong approximations even when DNNF is nondeterministic. The pinned entry specifies the uniform counting case and fixed 0 < ε < 1. The paper obtains one simple cubic 3-connected graph family, for every fixed 0 ≤ ε < 1, on the same original variables. - Lemma 33's proof, printed p. 60 (PDF p. 72), supplies the cubic 3-connected family of linear treewidth. The proof of Theorem 5, printed p. 44 (PDF p. 56), chooses a hard cut for each offered vtree. Lemmas 18, 21 and 22 justify the small exact rectangles on it. The proof of Theorem 12 reuses the same statement. The release derives a safe constant via the greedy independent-set bound |crossing|/4 and makes no claim about the stronger numerical value printed in Theorem 12's proof. Theorem 7, printed pp. 37–38 (PDF pp. 49–50), is the adversarial rectangle cover construction for ordinary DNNF and any subset of its accepting assignments. ## Primary comparators

- De Colnet and Mengel's *Lower Bounds for Approximate Knowledge Compilation* (IJCAI–PRICAI 2020), §§4–5, treats deterministic DNNF. Its code-based hard families and block/core rectangles already support closely related arguments with a disjoint cover. The thesis points out that in nondeterministic circuits the false-positive sets may overlap. The candidate controls each individual mixed rectangle by the full false-positive budget and only sums coverage of true points. [Proceedings](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2020/254) · [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13721)
- De Colnet and Mengel's *Characterizing Tseitin-formulas with short regular resolution refutations* (JAIR 2023), §3, proves the exact bounded-degree DNNF lower bound and supplies the hard-cut mechanism. Itsykson, Riazanov and Smirnov's *Tight Bounds for Tseitin Formulas* (SAT 2022), Theorem 4.1, proves the exact lower bound for every satisfiable Tseitin formula; §4.2 gives the stronger per-vtree exact rectangle bound for 3-connected graphs even without a degree cap. De Colnet, Szeider and Zhang's *Compilation and Fast Model Counting beyond CNF* uses the same adversarial method for exact constraints, including further families of equations over finite fields. Those appearances of the method cover exact representations. [JAIR authors' version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09609) · [SAT 2022](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SAT.2022.6) · [Beyond CNF, §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.00434v1)
- Pipatsrisawat and Darwiche's *A Lower Bound on the Size of Decomposable Negation Normal Form* (AAAI 2010) supplies the structured-DNNF lower-bound framework. Bova, Capelli, Mengel and Slivovsky's *Knowledge Compilation Meets Communication Complexity* (IJCAI 2016), §5, Theorem 13, states the exact fixed-vtree-cut rectangle cover bound for structured DNNF. Determinism provides disjointness as an extra property. This theorem applies to the accepting function even if that function only approximates the code. [AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/7600) · [IJCAI](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2016/147)
- Kashyap's *On Minimal Tree Realizations of Linear Codes*, §2 and §6, Eqs. (23)–(24), gives state-space dimensions and branchwidth via minimal tree realizations. The compatible syndrome states and the resulting exact structured circuit are a direct construction in that tradition. The companion's overlap-tolerant lower bounds concern approximation by ordinary circuits with no common vtree, or by structured circuits at a fixed vtree cut. [Kashyap preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1383)

The source check included the thesis, its related deterministic and exact results, later exact compilation papers, rank/syndrome tree realizations and the structured rectangle-cover literature. This establishes the comparison with the checked results. General distributions, changed variable sets, and optimal exponents for all affine codes remain outside the release's claims.
